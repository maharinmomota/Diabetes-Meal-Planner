Blood Sugar Adaptive Meal Planning

Enhanced Algorithm: Automatically adjusts carb limits and food preferences based on your current blood sugar level.

  • Low Blood Sugar (<70): Increases carbs, prefers medium-high GI foods for quick energy
  • Normal (70-140): Standard carb targets, balanced GI food selection
  • High (140-250): Reduces carbs 25-50%, strongly favors low GI foods
  • Very High (>250): Minimal carbs, protein-focused with low GI vegetables
  • Glycemic Index (GI): Algorithm weights food selection based on how quickly they raise blood sugar
